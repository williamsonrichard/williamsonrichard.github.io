Gjentakelse — Records giving rise to records

Research

notes upon records; copies of, or references to, records; etc.

Example

notes by Knut Olborg (Norwegian historian) on an archive of self-administration of interned German soldiers in Norway in 1945-47. (AV/RA-PA-2101/F)

Knut Olborg began his research on the material and systematically went through it, box for box. These notes can be useful for those who wish to work with this German-language and difficult to access archival material.
